The Hook

Tuesday belongs to members. The lounge is closed to the public that entire day, so the full 9 AM to 5 PM window is member time on every rig. Monday, Wednesday and Thursday add a member-only morning before public hours start, and Friday and Saturday give members access right through to 9 PM.

A membership is built for people who want to drive often without booking a session every time. Instead of paying the drop-in rate each visit, you get a set number of hours a month at a member rate, extended weekday access on top of standard lounge hours, and the run of the full car and track library, over 170 cars across more than 140 tracks, on any of our rigs: the Motion GT flagship, a triple-screen cockpit, or one of the manual, H-pattern rigs.

What Every Tier Gets You

Rate

A $25 an hour member rate across every tier, a savings of $15 to $35 an hour off our $40-$60 drop-in rate depending on rig. Discounted rates also apply to time booked beyond your included hours.

Access

Monday to Thursday 9 AM to 5 PM, Friday 9 AM to 9 PM and Saturday 2 PM to 9 PM, on top of standard lounge hours. Tuesday is entirely member hours, since the lounge is closed to the public that day.

Freedom

Full access to the car and track library, 170+ cars across 140+ tracks. Set up and drive whatever you want instead of a fixed session plan.

Career

Use your own iRacing account and log consistent seat time on the Motion GT and our other pro rigs to build toward real iRacing career progress.

The Fine Print

  1. Six month minimum

    Memberships run on a minimum six-month commitment, built for real, consistent seat time rather than a single month of driving. That's a six-month total of $300 on Starter, $600 on Bronze, $1,200 on Silver, or $1,800 on Gold.

  2. Training first

    One simulator training session is required before independent, unsupervised use.

  3. Your own iRacing account

    Membership sessions run on your own iRacing account, so your progress and career carry over between visits.

Membership FAQ

What does an Academy Sim Racing membership include?

Every tier includes a set number of monthly hours, a discounted member rate, extended weekday lounge access, and full access to the car and track library, over 170 cars across more than 140 tracks. Higher tiers add guest session time so you can bring someone along.

How much does a membership cost?

Four tiers: Starter is $50 a month for 2 hours, Bronze is $100 a month for 4 hours plus a 30 minute guest session, Silver is $200 a month for 8 hours plus a 1 hour guest session, and Gold is $300 a month for 12 hours plus 4 hours of guest sessions. All four work out to a $25 per hour member rate.

What are member hours at the lounge?

Member access runs Monday to Thursday 9 AM to 5 PM, Friday 9 AM to 9 PM and Saturday 2 PM to 9 PM, on top of standard lounge hours. Tuesday is the one day the lounge is closed to the public entirely, so that full 9 AM to 5 PM window belongs to members only.

Is there a minimum commitment?

Yes. Memberships run on a minimum six month commitment, and a simulator training session is required before independent, unsupervised use. You'll need your own iRacing account for racing sessions.

How does the member rate compare to a drop-in session?

A drop-in session runs $40 to $60 an hour depending on the rig. The membership works out to $25 an hour, a savings of $15 to $35 an hour for a member using their included time.

How do I sign up?

Pick a tier and join through the Academy Membership product on the Academy Sim Racing store, or visit member.academysim.com for full tier details and the member portal.
