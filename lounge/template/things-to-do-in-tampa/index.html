What It Actually Is

This is not an arcade and it is not VR. Each rig, meaning the seat, wheel, pedals and screens together, has a direct drive steering wheel, a proper pedal set and screens filling your vision, running iRacing and Assetto Corsa. Most have a fixed cockpit. One, the Motion GT, sits on a platform that moves.

The wheel pushes back at you the way a real one does. The wheel loads up as the front tires start to slide, you feel the curbs at the edge of the track, and on the motion rig the braking and cornering come through the seat as well. It is closer to driving than to playing a game.

The important part for a first visit is that none of it requires skill on arrival. Staff get first-timers set up and comfortable, with a car and a circuit chosen to suit you. Most people are setting competitive laps inside their first session.

Who It Works For

Date Night

Two rigs side by side, head to head, with a lap timer settling the argument. It is more interesting than dinner and considerably more competitive. Book two hours if you want it to be the whole evening rather than the start of it.

Groups And Birthdays

Birthday parties are one of our most popular bookings, and kids race regularly. For a bigger group you can take the whole lounge, all eight rigs, nobody else in the room.

A Rainy Afternoon

Florida weather does what it does. This is indoors, which in a Florida summer matters more than it sounds.

Work And Team Events

Corporate teams book the lounge for the same reason friend groups do. Everyone can drive, the lap times end up close enough to be competitive, and nobody has to be good at it.

Actual Racing Fans

If you watch F1, IMSA or NASCAR, you can drive the same real-world circuits you watch on Sundays, in cars of the same type.

On Your Own

Plenty of people book solo, concentrate hard for an hour and leave. Coaching is available if you want to actually get quicker.

What A First Visit Looks Like

  1. You turn up and get set up

    Seat position, wheel, pedals, and a car and circuit chosen to suit you. There is nothing to bring and nothing to wear specially.

  2. You do a few laps badly

    Everyone does. The first few laps are for working out where the circuit goes and how hard you can lean on the car.

  3. It clicks

    Somewhere in the first twenty minutes the car stops feeling random. This is the part people come back for.

  4. You start chasing the clock

    Once you are consistent, the lap timer becomes the point. If you booked with friends, this is where it gets loud.

How Long To Book

An hour is enough to try it. Two hours is better if you are coming as a couple or a group, because you spend the first part learning and the second part racing.
