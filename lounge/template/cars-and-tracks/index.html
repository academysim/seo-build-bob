Not Sure What To Pick

  1. First time in a simulator

    Start with GT3. The cars have traction control and anti-lock brakes, they shift with paddles so there is no gearbox to think about, and they are forgiving enough that a first session is spent driving rather than spinning. Pair it with a circuit you have seen on television.

  2. You drive a manual road car and want to use it

    Four of our eight rigs have an H-pattern shifter and a clutch pedal, including the Motion GT. Classic race cars and drift cars are the natural fit. Mention it when you book so we put you on the right rig.

  3. You have a real track day coming up

    Pick the circuit first and the car second. Our track guides cover what each circuit asks of a driver, and learning the layout in advance is the part that transfers best.

  4. You want the hardest thing here

    Formula cars on a circuit you do not know. No traction control in most classes, very little weight, and aerodynamic load that rises sharply with speed. It is the steepest learning curve we offer.
