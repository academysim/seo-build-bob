The Idea Is Simple

CONFIDENCE BEFORE THE CAR

Show your teen the fundamentals in a calm, repeatable setting first. Mistakes cost nothing here, so they show up to the real car already prepared.

01

START ON THE SIM

Steering, braking, mirrors, lane position and turns, all learned in a real-feel cockpit without exposing the family car to a beginner's mistakes.

02

PRACTICE THE HARD STUFF

Highway merges, emergency braking, parking, rain, fog, night driving, and distracted-driving scenarios, all repeated as many times as it takes.

03

HIT THE ROAD READY

Your teen arrives at real lessons calmer and more capable. Parents watch progress the whole way, the single biggest factor in teen-driver safety.

What To Expect

YOUR FIRST SESSION

  1. Arrive & get seatedWe fit the seat, wheel and pedals so the cockpit feels like a real car.
  2. Controls walkthroughSteering, braking, mirrors and scanning, all before anything moves.
  3. Basic vehicle controlSmooth inputs in an empty environment. No traffic, no pressure.
  4. Build upTurns, lane position, then traffic and hazards as confidence grows.
  5. Debrief with the parentWhat went well, what to practice next, and where to go from here.

PARENTS ARE WELCOME TO WATCH

You can stay for the whole session and see exactly what your teen is practicing, and where they still need work. Parental involvement is one of the strongest factors in teen-driver safety.

WHO COACHES

Sessions are run by Academy simulator coaches. Academy is not a state-licensed driving school and does not employ state-certified driving instructors, so simulator time supplements the behind-the-wheel instruction Florida requires rather than counting toward it.

PROGRESS REPORTING
