About Academy

BUILT BY DRIVERS.
RUN BY A VETERAN.

Academy Driver's Ed is the driver-training arm of Academy Sim Racing in Tampa, a veteran-owned simulator lounge, custom simulator builder, and driver training center.

WHY WE DO THIS

Most new drivers learn every fundamental for the first time in live traffic, with a nervous parent in the passenger seat. That is a bad place to make a first mistake.

We already had professional simulators, the space, and people who coach drivers for a living. Pointing that at new-driver training was obvious, and the response from parents and driver-education teachers is why it became its own program.

Academy is a Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business founded by a former Army Ranger. The same standard applies here as anywhere else we have worked: train the hard parts before they count.

Meet the Team
An honest note: simulator training supplements real-world driving instruction. It does not replace required behind-the-wheel experience, and it is not a state-licensed driver-education course. Research shows simulators improve hazard awareness, lane maintenance and speed control; we use them to make real road time safer and more productive.
