Why Simulators

BUILT FOR SAFER TRAINING

The same advantages that make simulators standard in aviation and motorsport now apply to teaching students to drive.

SAFER TRAINING

Simulate hydroplaning, emergency braking, and distracted driving without exposing students to on-road traffic.

MORE REPEATABLE PRACTICE

Students repeat the identical exercise as many times as it takes, and instructors give immediate feedback on hazard awareness, lane control, and speed.

SCALABLE FOR CLASSROOMS

Run multiple students through identical, consistent training scenarios, no two-students-per-car bottleneck.

LOWER LONG-TERM COSTS

Reduce reliance on vehicle fleets, fuel, maintenance, and insurance over the life of the program.

INCLUSIVE FOR ALL

Customizable environments support students of every skill level and a range of learning needs.

PARENT VISIBILITY

Families see skill progression and share feedback. Parental involvement measurably improves teen safety.

For Florida Schools

DRIVER ED IS CHANGING

Florida now asks driver-education programs to go well beyond the basics, and a car in a parking lot cannot safely replicate most of it.

The Problem

WHAT'S NOW REQUIRED

The Florida Department of Education expects programs to teach:

  • Hazard recognition and risk assessment
  • Defensive driving strategies
  • Decision-making in real-world scenarios
  • Awareness of distraction, fatigue, and impairment
  • Safe interaction with traffic, pedestrians, and cyclists

Traditional, behind-the-wheel-only instruction cannot safely replicate these situations.

The Solution

ACADEMY'S TRAINING SYSTEM

Professional-grade driving simulators built specifically for schools, districts, and training programs across Florida. Academy can service qualifying programs statewide; see district-specific pages for Hillsborough, Pasco, Pinellas, Polk, Manatee and Sarasota counties.

  • Realistic vehicle dynamics and feedback
  • Scenario-based training: weather, hazards, traffic
  • Instructor-controlled environments
  • Performance tracking and repeatable drills

Train students for real-world driving, without exposing them to real-world traffic.
