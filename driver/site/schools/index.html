The Case

Why a simulator, and why now

Driver education has a practical problem that has nothing to do with willingness. Behind the wheel instruction needs a car, an instructor and a road, which caps how many students can be trained and makes some situations impossible to teach at all.

A simulator does not solve all of that. What it does is let a student experience a hazard, a skid or a wet-road stop without any of the risk, and let them do it repeatedly. It also lets more students get hands-on time in a fixed window, because a simulator does not need a public road.

What a simulator can genuinely do

  • Expose students to hazards that cannot be staged safely on a road
  • Let a student repeat the same situation until the response is automatic
  • Give hands-on time to more students within a school day
  • Standardize what every student experiences, rather than depending on what came up on the day
  • Run in a gym, a classroom or a media center, in any weather

What it cannot do

It does not replace behind the wheel instruction and it does not satisfy any state licensing or driver education requirement. We would rather say that plainly than have a district discover it after a purchase order.

Please note: Academy Sim Racing does not currently have simulators installed in Florida school districts. The programs described here are services Academy offers and can deploy. Any reference to a district on this site describes an area we can serve, not an existing installation or partnership.

What Academy is, and is not. Academy Sim Racing supplies racing and driving simulators and runs staffed programs with them. We are not a licensed driving school and we do not employ state-certified driver education instructors. Nothing we provide counts toward Florida licensing requirements, the supervised driving hours a learner must complete, or any state-required driver education course. Districts and schools should deliver those through their own certified staff or an appropriately licensed provider. What we offer is supplementary practice and hazard exposure alongside them.

Delivery

Which one suits you depends on how many students and how long you have.

01

Mobile program

We bring the simulators to your campus, run the sessions and take everything away again. The most common request, and the easiest to arrange.

02

Longer-term solutions

Where a district wants a permanent capability, we can discuss what that looks like. This is a conversation rather than a catalog item.

Practical

What we need to know

Numbers and time

How many students, and over how long. Those two numbers determine everything else, including how many simulators make sense and whether one day is realistic.

The space

A gym, cafeteria, media center or large classroom all work. We need floor space and mains power at the setup point, and a way to get equipment in from a vehicle.

Who is running it

Our staff operate the simulators. Your driver education teacher or SRO usually leads the instructional side, and we work to their plan rather than replacing it.

Procurement

Tell us early how your district buys things. Quotes, W-9s, insurance certificates and vendor registration all take longer than the scheduling does.

Questions

What districts ask

Do you have simulators in Florida schools already?
No. Academy does not currently have simulators installed in any school district. These are programs we offer and can deploy, and we would rather be straight about that.
Does this replace behind the wheel instruction?
No. It also does not satisfy any state licensing or driver education requirement. It supplements classroom and behind the wheel instruction rather than substituting for either.
How many students can go through in a day?
It depends on session length and how many simulators. Tell us your numbers and the window and we will give you a realistic figure rather than an optimistic one.
What space do we need?
A gym, cafeteria, media center or large classroom. We need floor space, mains power and a way to get equipment in.
Who supervises the students?
Our staff run the simulators. School staff supervise students, as they would for any outside provider on campus.
How do we start a conversation?
Contact us with the school or district, rough student numbers and what you are trying to achieve. We will send options and costs.

Talk to us about your program

Tell us the school or district, roughly how many students and what you are trying to achieve. We will come back with options and costs.
